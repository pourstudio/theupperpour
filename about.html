My Story

About Us — The Upper Pour

Est. 2024

"The Upper Pour started in our small kitchen at home."

Where it began

I really enjoyed making coffee and trying different flavors during my free time. My friends noticed how passionate I was about it, and they encouraged me to start my own coffee business instead of just making drinks for ourselves.

Faith & purpose

Aside from making coffee, I am also an active youth leader at Jesus Hope of the World Church – Pasig . My experience in discipleship inspired me a lot when it comes to serving people and doing things with purpose.

Through church, I learned how important it is to connect with people and make them feel welcomed. Now, I want to serve people not only through sharing the gospel, but also through coffee that I personally make and enjoy creating.

What it became

The Upper Pour is a small passion project that turned into something real because of support from friends, faith, and love for coffee.
